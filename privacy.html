Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

God is a personal app used only by its owner. This policy covers the data it reads from Google when the owner connects their Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, God reads and writes the owner's Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. It requests nothing else.

Where the data goes

The data is shown in the app and kept on the owner's own computers. The sign-in token is stored on those computers only. Nothing is sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for advertising.

Google API data

God's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/iustinvaliza-del/god-app.
